These projects are my own work outside the curriculum: models I built to understand a problem properly, checked against published data.

Nov 2026 · mission analysis · pykep

Screening multi-asteroid tours

Cheap formulas for ranking asteroid-to-asteroid transfers miss their own best candidates, because similar orbits drift into phase over centuries. I added a phasing correction with no fitted parameters and tested it against exact Lambert transfers on ESA's GTOC7 catalogue of 16,000 asteroids.
